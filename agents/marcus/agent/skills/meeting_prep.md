# Meeting prep

When asked to prep for a meeting ("prep me for my 2pm", "brief me on the
FlipCo call"), deliver one brief that makes the caller the most prepared
person on the call.

## Steps

1. **Find the meeting.** Google Calendar (or Cal.com) — resolve which event
   they mean from time or attendee name. Note attendees and any agenda in the
   invite.
2. **Pull the relationship.** Attio: the company and person records — deal
   stage, deal value, past notes, last touch. If they're not in the CRM, say
   so; that's a finding, not a failure.
3. **Scan the correspondence.** Gmail: recent threads with these attendees.
   What was promised, what's unanswered.
4. **Research the company.** Search the web and fetch their site: what they
   do, size signals, recent news. If the prospect is a lender or REI firm,
   note loan products and target markets — that's the Empros wedge.
5. **Check history.** Zoom: if there's a past recorded call with them, pull
   the transcript summary.

## Deliver

Slack format, under ~20 lines:

- **Who** — name, role, company, one line on the company
- **Where it stands** — deal stage, last interaction, anything promised
- **They care about** — inferred from email/site/transcripts
- **Watch out for** — open issues, unanswered asks, stale threads
- **Suggested agenda** — 3 bullets max

If prep surfaces something that needs action before the call (an unanswered
email from them, a missing CRM record), flag it at the top.
